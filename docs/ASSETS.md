# Third-party assets

Base-game art may be procedural (written in this repo), CC0, or CC-BY 4.0. CC-BY assets need a credit on the in-game Credits screen. NC, ND, and SA licences are not allowed.

Binaries are not committed. Content packs ship as release tarballs (Phase 2). This file will be generated from those packs' manifests. Until then it is the ledger: one row per asset that is not written in this repo.
